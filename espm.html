Frequently Asked Questions

Should I back up my ESPM data?

Yes. While ESPM is continuing under the EPA, having an independent backup protects against accidental loss, access issues, or workflow disruptions. As an official ESPM Partner, Scaler provides a secure, automated backup solution.

How often does Scaler backup ESPM data?

Daily, via the Scaler Data Collection Portal.

What is the backup process?

Email info@scalerglobal.com to get started. Scaler provides an NDA and walks you through sharing ESPM assets. Once shared, the team syncs your meters and grants access to the Collection Portal.

How much does it cost?

Backup, storage, and access are free.

Do I have access to my data?

You own your data and retain full access via the collection portal. You can export data anytime.

Can Scaler support integration to source data?

Yes. Scaler offers direct API integrations to third-party data providers, utility providers, and via the Scaler API. Utility data tools include bill scraping and automated utility syncing.
